    How This Finder Works

    The finder matches your birth month and day against the standard tropical zodiac date ranges shown in the table above. No birth year, account or server is needed, so your answer appears instantly on your own device. If your birthday is within one day of a boundary — a cusp date — the page flags it, because in those cases the Sun's exact changeover time in your birth year decides the sign.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is my zodiac sign?

    Your zodiac sign — also called your Sun sign — is the sign the Sun was in on your birthday. Pick your birth month and day above and this finder shows it instantly, along with its dates, element, ruling planet and traits. You do not need your birth year or birth time for the Sun sign in most cases.

    What if I was born on the cusp?

    If your birthday falls within a day of a sign boundary, you were born on the cusp. Dates alone cannot settle the exact Sun sign in cusp cases, because the Sun changes sign at a specific time that varies by year. You may recognise traits of both neighbouring signs. For an exact answer, calculate your full birth chart with your birth year, time and place.

    Is the zodiac sign the same as the Sun sign?

    Yes, in everyday use. When people ask “what is my zodiac sign?” they almost always mean the Sun sign. A full birth chart also has a Moon sign, a Rising sign (Ascendant) and planet signs, which can be different from your Sun sign.

    Do I need my birth time?

    No — not for your Sun sign, except in cusp cases where the exact time and year matter. You do need an accurate birth time for your Rising sign and houses, which is why the full Birth Chart Calculator asks for it.

    Go Deeper Than Your Sun Sign

    Your Sun sign is the starting point, not the whole picture. Your complete natal chart shows your Moon, Rising, every planet and the houses.

How It Works

This calculator uses the standard component method, stated plainly so you can verify it: (1) reduce the birth month to a single digit, keeping a master number 11 or 22 whole; (2) reduce the birth day the same way; (3) add the four digits of the birth year together and reduce that total, again keeping 11 or 22; (4) add the three results together and reduce to a single digit 1–9 — unless the total is a master number 11, 22 or 33, which is kept. Some other calculators add every digit of the date in one string and can reach a different answer; the method matters, which is why this page shows its working in full.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are master numbers in numerology?

Master numbers are 11, 22 and 33. In the method this calculator uses, they are not reduced to a single digit at the final step, because they are traditionally read as stronger, more demanding versions of their root numbers: 11 carries the energy of 2, 22 carries the energy of 4, and 33 carries the energy of 6. At the month, day and year stage, 11 and 22 are also kept whole instead of being reduced.

Which Life Path method does this calculator use?

This calculator uses the standard component method, stated plainly: first reduce the birth month on its own (keeping 11 or 22), then reduce the birth day on its own (keeping 11 or 22), then add the digits of the birth year and reduce that result (keeping 11 or 22). Add the three results together and reduce the total to 1–9, keeping 11, 22 or 33 if the total lands on one. The page shows every step of the arithmetic for your date, so you can check it yourself.

Does my Life Path number ever change?

No. Your Life Path number comes only from your date of birth, and your birth date never changes, so your Life Path number stays the same for your whole life. Numbers that do change each year — such as your Personal Year number — are calculated from your birth month and day combined with the current calendar year, which is a different calculation.

Why does another calculator give me a different Life Path number?

Usually because it uses a different adding method. Some calculators add every digit of the full birth date in one long string, which can produce a different result — especially where a master number appears in one part of the date. Neither approach is a data error; they are different traditions of adding. This page uses the component method described above and shows its full working, so you can always see exactly how your number was reached.

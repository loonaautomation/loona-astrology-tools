How It Works

Each letter is converted with the Pythagorean chart below: A, J and S are 1; B, K and T are 2; and so on up to I and R at 9. The Destiny number adds every letter. The Soul Urge adds only the vowels A, E, I, O and U — on this page Y counts as a consonant. The Personality number adds only the consonants. Each total is reduced by adding its digits until one digit remains, except that master numbers 11 and 22 are kept when they appear during reduction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which name should I use — my birth name or my current name?

Numerology convention uses your full birth name — the name given at birth, exactly as it was first recorded — for the Destiny, Soul Urge and Personality numbers, because those numbers are said to describe your life path from birth. If your name has changed through marriage or by choice, many numerologists also calculate the new name separately to see the influence it adds, but the birth name remains the foundation.

What are master numbers 11 and 22?

Master numbers are 11 and 22. In this calculator a total that reduces through 11 or 22 is kept and shown instead of being reduced straight to 2 or 4, because tradition gives master numbers extra intensity and potential. They are considered more demanding as well as more gifted, and this page always tells you when one appears in your result.

Does the letter Y count as a vowel or a consonant?

On this page Y always counts as a consonant. That is the rule this calculator states and uses consistently: the vowels are A, E, I, O and U only. Some other systems count Y as a vowel when it sounds like one, which can change a Soul Urge result — so if you compare calculators, check which Y rule each one uses.

What do Destiny, Soul Urge and Personality numbers mean?

The Destiny (Expression) number uses all the letters of your full birth name and describes your natural talents and life direction. The Soul Urge uses only the vowels and describes your inner desires — what truly motivates you. The Personality number uses only the consonants and describes the outer impression you make on others. Together they give a fuller picture than any one number alone.

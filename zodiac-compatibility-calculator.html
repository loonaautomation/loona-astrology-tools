      How to Use This Calculator

      1. Select the first person's zodiac sign (their Sun sign — find it with the Zodiac Sign Finder if you are not sure).
      2. Select the second person's sign and press Calculate Compatibility.
      3. Read the overall score first, then the love, friendship and work mini-scores — many pairs are strong in one area and weaker in another.
      4. Finish with the strengths and challenges lists: they are the most useful part, because they name what to appreciate and what to handle with care.

      How the Score Works

      Every sign belongs to one element: Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) or Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces). The overall score comes only from the two elements: the same sign scores 88, two signs of the same element score 92, Fire with Air and Earth with Water score 85, Air with Water scores 62, Earth with Air scores 58, Fire with Earth scores 55, and Fire with Water scores 45. The score is symmetric — Aries with Leo scores exactly the same as Leo with Aries.

      The mini-scores are derived from the overall score with fixed, transparent rules: Love equals the overall score; Friendship is the overall score plus 5 (capped at 100), minus 7 for a Fire and Water pair, whose social rhythms differ most; Work is the overall score minus 5, plus 6 when an Earth sign is involved, because Earth brings follow-through to a team.

      An Honest Note About Compatibility Scores

      This calculator compares Sun signs only. It cannot see either person's Moon sign, Venus, Mars, Rising sign or full chart — all of which astrologers weigh heavily in real compatibility work — and it knows nothing about your actual history together. Use the result for entertainment and general guidance: a starting point for a good conversation, never a verdict on a relationship and never a reason to start or end one.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      How accurate is zodiac compatibility?

      Treat it as general guidance and entertainment, not a prediction. This calculator compares Sun signs only, using the four elements. A real relationship depends on both full birth charts — especially the Moon, Venus and Mars signs — and far more on communication, values and effort. A high score does not guarantee a good relationship, and a low score never rules one out.

      Which zodiac signs are the best matches?

      In this element-based system the strongest matches share an element (92%) — such as Aries with Leo or Taurus with Virgo — or pair Fire with Air and Earth with Water (85%), because those element pairs feed each other. The full 12 by 12 grid below shows the score for every possible pair.

      What do the elements mean for compatibility?

      Fire signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius) are energetic and direct; Earth signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn) are practical and steady; Air signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) are communicative and idea-driven; Water signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces) are emotional and intuitive. Signs that share an element tend to want similar things from a relationship, which is why they score highest here.

      Can a low-scoring pair still have a great relationship?

      Yes — often. Challenging pairings such as Fire with Water (45%) or Fire with Earth (55%) ask both people to learn a different style: passion meeting sensitivity, or spontaneity meeting caution. Many long, happy relationships sit in the lower half of this grid. The score describes the starting chemistry of two Sun signs, nothing more.

Honest Notes: Time Matters, and Unknown Time Means No Result

Your result depends on your exact birth time. The Ascendant changes sign roughly every 2 hours and moves about one degree every four minutes, so a birth time that is wrong by 10–15 minutes can change the Rising sign if you were born near a boundary. This calculator uses the same Equal House engine as our Birth Chart Calculator, so the two pages always agree with each other.

If you do not know your birth time, this calculator will not fake a result — the time field is required. Use the Birth Chart Calculator instead: it can show your Sun, Moon and planet signs from your date and place, and honestly hides the Rising sign until a real birth time is known.

How It Works

The calculator converts your local birth time to UTC using the UTC offset (and daylight-saving adjustment) for your birth place, then computes the Ascendant — the ecliptic degree rising over the eastern horizon at that moment and location — with the same astronomy engine as our Birth Chart Calculator (cities.js for the place lookup and astro.js for the calculation). The sign containing that degree is your Rising sign. Everything runs with JavaScript on your own device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does the Rising sign need my exact birth time?

The Ascendant moves through all 12 signs in about 24 hours, so it changes sign roughly every 2 hours, and it moves about one degree every four minutes. A birth time that is out by even 10–15 minutes can put the Ascendant in the neighbouring sign if you were born near a boundary. That is why this calculator requires your birth time and does not guess one: without a time, there is no honest Rising sign to show.

What is the difference between my Rising sign and my Sun sign?

Your Sun sign comes from your birth date alone and describes your core identity and direction. Your Rising sign — the Ascendant — is the sign that was coming up over the eastern horizon at your exact birth moment and place. It is traditionally read as the face you show the world: your first impression, your manner and your appearance. The two are often different signs, and both are part of your full birth chart.

What if I was born near a Rising sign boundary?

If your Ascendant degree is close to 0 or 30 degrees of its sign, a small error in your recorded birth time — even 10–15 minutes — could move it into the neighbouring sign. In that case, treat the result as approximate, check the birth time on your birth certificate if you can, and read the neighbouring sign's description too. This calculator uses the same Equal House engine as our Birth Chart Calculator, so both pages will agree.

What if I do not know my birth time?

Then this calculator cannot give you a Rising sign, and it will not invent one — the time input is required. You can still use our Birth Chart Calculator, which calculates your Sun sign, Moon sign and planet signs from your birth date and place and honestly hides the Rising sign and houses when the time is unknown. If you can find your birth time on a birth certificate or hospital record, come back and your Rising sign will take under a minute.

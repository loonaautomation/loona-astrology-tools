How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your birth date and, as closely as you can, your birth time.
  2. Search for your birth city — latitude, longitude and UTC offset fill in automatically — or type those three values yourself.
  3. Tick the daylight-saving box only if clocks were one hour ahead on your birth date in that place.
  4. Press Find My Moon Sign. Your local birth time is converted to UTC and the Moon's position is computed for that exact moment.

How It Works

This page uses the same verified astronomy engine as the Birth Chart Calculator. Your local birth time is converted to UTC using the offset you provide (plus one hour if daylight saving applied), and the engine computes the Moon's ecliptic longitude for that moment, including its major perturbation terms. The sign is the 30-degree sector containing that longitude, and the degree shown is the Moon's position inside the sign.

An Honest Note About Birth Time

The Moon moves about 13 degrees a day — fast enough to change sign roughly every two and a half days. If you do not know your birth time, this calculator uses noon and also checks the Moon sign at 00:00 and 23:59 of your birth day. If those two differ, your Moon changed sign that day and only a real birth time can settle the answer — the result says so openly instead of guessing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my birth time matter for my Moon sign?

The Moon moves about 13 degrees a day — roughly one full sign every two and a half days, and about half a degree an hour. If the Moon changed sign on your birth date, only your exact birth time can say which side of the boundary you were born on. That is why this calculator asks for your time and place, and why it warns you when the Moon changed sign during your birth day.

What is the difference between my Moon sign and my Sun sign?

Your Sun sign is the sign the Sun was in on your birthday; it changes about once a month and describes your core identity and direction. Your Moon sign is where the Moon was at your exact birth moment; astrologers link it to your emotions, instincts, habits and what makes you feel safe. Many people recognise themselves in their Moon sign's description of their private, unguarded self.

What if I do not know my birth time?

Tick the box and the calculator uses 12:00 (noon) local time. It also checks the Moon sign at the very start and end of your birth day: if the Moon stayed in one sign all day, your result is secure; if it changed sign that day, the page tells you plainly and shows both possibilities, because the true sign depends on the hour you were born.

How accurate is this Moon sign calculator?

Positions are calculated in your browser with the same astronomy engine as our Birth Chart Calculator (Schlyter's method, with the Moon's major perturbation terms). The Moon's longitude is typically accurate to a few arc-minutes. A birth moment within about half a degree of a sign boundary can still fall in the neighbouring sign in some professional ephemerides, so treat exact-boundary results with care.

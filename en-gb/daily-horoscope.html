How Your Daily Horoscope Is Made

Honestly — because you deserve to know. This page does not phone a server, track you, or pretend to calculate anything personal. Your reading is picked from a bank of written templates using a fixed seed made of two things only: your sign and the date.

  • Same sign + same date = same reading. The seed produces the same picks on every device, so you and a friend with the same sign see identical text today. That is by design, and it is why we never call this personalised.
  • It rotates daily. Change the date and the seed changes, so tomorrow's reading is different. Use the Yesterday and Tomorrow buttons to see the rotation for yourself.
  • No personal data, no server. Your birth date, if you type one, is used only to find your sign and never leaves your device. There is nothing to sign up for because there is nothing to store.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Loona Daily Horoscope free?

Yes, completely. Every sign's full daily reading — overview, love, career, money, energy, lucky number and lucky colour — is free, with no premium version and no paywall.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is no account system at all. Pick your sign and your reading appears instantly. If you choose, your sign is remembered only on your own device so it is ready next time — nothing is sent to us.

Why is my reading the same as my friend's with the same sign?

Because that is how this horoscope honestly works. Each day's reading is generated from a fixed seed made of your sign and the date, so everyone with the same sign sees exactly the same reading that day, on any device. It is not personalised, and we do not pretend otherwise.

When does today's horoscope change?

At midnight, your local time. The reading is seeded by the date on your own device, so a new day brings a new reading automatically — and you can peek at yesterday or tomorrow with the day buttons.

Is a horoscope advice I should follow?

No. Horoscopes are entertainment, not advice. Enjoy your reading as a light moment in your day, and make real decisions about love, work and money on real information, not on a star sign.

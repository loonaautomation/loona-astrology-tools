How This Calculator Works

The calculator converts your local birth time to UTC using the offset (and daylight-saving adjustment) you provide, then computes the Sun, Moon and planet longitudes, the Ascendant and Midheaven for your latitude and longitude, and Equal House placements — all with JavaScript on your own device.

Positions are calculated in your browser with a compact astronomy method. A planet within about 3° of a sign boundary may fall in the neighbouring sign in some professional ephemerides.

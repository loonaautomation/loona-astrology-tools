Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between modern and traditional birthstones?

The modern list was standardised in 1912 by the American National Retail Jewelers Association and is the list most jewellers use today. The traditional list is older, drawn from historical and cultural sources over centuries. Some months have the same stone on both lists, while others — such as March, August and November — differ, which is why this finder always shows you both.

Why do some months have more than one birthstone?

Some months have several stones because the lists grew over time and because some original stones were rare or expensive. June has Pearl and Alexandrite, August has Peridot and Spinel, October has Opal and Tourmaline, and December has Turquoise, Tanzanite, Zircon and Blue Topaz on the modern list. Having a choice lets you pick the stone, colour and price that suits you best.

Is a birthstone the same as a zodiac stone?

No. A birthstone is linked to your birth month — everyone born in June shares Pearl as a modern birthstone. A zodiac stone is linked to your zodiac sign, and a month usually spans two signs: June, for example, covers Gemini and Cancer, which have different zodiac associations. This page shows both your birthstones and the zodiac signs for your month so you can compare them.

Which birthstone list should I use?

Use whichever you prefer — there is no single correct list. The modern list is the safest choice for buying jewellery, because jewellers stock and label stones by it. The traditional list is a lovely choice if you value history or family custom. Many people simply choose the stone whose colour or meaning they like most.
